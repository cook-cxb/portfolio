I study how small a bird-sound classifier can get and still keep listening on solar power.

Current research

How far can an environmental or bird sound classifier be compressed to fit a microcontroller-class budget?

And can a simple energy-aware switch between a tiny and a larger model keep it running through a simulated solar-battery day and night cycle?

The budget

The target is a microcontroller-class device, so the model has to fit inside a hard limit on memory and storage.

RAMabout 256 KB
Flashabout 1 MB

Bars are drawn to scale against each other (1 MB is four times 256 KB).

Two questions, one system

Compress

Shrink a sound classifier step by step and measure how much accuracy is lost at each size.

Switch

Use a tiny model when the battery is low and a larger one when there is energy to spare.

An illustrative sketch of the idea, not measured results.
